About Fardeen

Fardeen PP is a video editor and videographer based in Calicut. He has a keen eye for storytelling and design, and keeps exploring new techniques so every project feels current.

He studied Multimedia Design at Vismayam College of Art and Media and holds a B.Com in Finance from MES College, Vadakara.

Questions and answers

Quick answers before you get in touch.

What kind of videos does Fardeen make?

Instagram reels, interviews, event films, social media videos and YouTube content.

Does he shoot as well as edit?

Yes. Fardeen works as a videographer and a video editor. He can also edit footage you already have.

Can he design posters and thumbnails?

Yes. He designs posters, thumbnails and Instagram creatives with Photoshop, Illustrator and CorelDRAW, so they match the video.

Which software does he use?

Premiere Pro, After Effects and DaVinci Resolve for editing, grading and motion. CapCut and VN for quick social edits, and Blender for 3D.

Where is he based?

Calicut, Kerala. Message him with the location and date if you need a shoot elsewhere.

Which languages does he work in?

Malayalam and English.

How do I start a project and get a price?

Send a message on WhatsApp with what you need, the length of the video and where it will be posted. Pricing depends on the type and length of the project, so ask for a quote in the same message.

Have a project in mind?

Send Fardeen a message with what you need and where the video will be posted.
